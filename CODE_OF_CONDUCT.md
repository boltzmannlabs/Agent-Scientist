# Code of Conduct

Agent Scientist is maintained by Boltzmann Labs. We want contributors, researchers,
students, and users to collaborate in a respectful, welcoming environment.

## Scope

This code applies to project-managed issues, pull requests, discussions, support
channels, events, and other spaces where people represent Agent Scientist.
It applies to maintainers as well as contributors and users.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.
- Welcome questions and explain technical feedback clearly and constructively.
- Critique ideas and code, not the person proposing them.
- Credit others' work and respect applicable licenses and attribution requirements.
- Distinguish evidence, computational predictions, and unverified claims.
- Protect confidential research, personal information, credentials, and unpublished work.
- Respect reasonable boundaries and stop unwanted contact when asked.

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment and intimidation are not.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, or sustained harassment.
- Sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualized content directed at others.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, confidential datasets, or private correspondence.
- Impersonation, spam, or repeated disruption of project spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.

Good-faith bug reports, scientific criticism, and disagreement with maintainers are
not misconduct by themselves.

## Reporting a concern

For the current internal distribution, contact the Boltzmann Labs maintainer who
provided your build through an existing private company channel. Include:

- Where and when the incident occurred.
- A short description of what happened.
- Relevant links or carefully redacted evidence, if safe to share.
- Any immediate safety concerns and your preferred way to receive a response.

Do not post reports containing personal or confidential information in public
issues. Do not send passwords, API keys, or unnecessary research data.
For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Before public release:** Boltzmann Labs must designate and publish a private
conduct-reporting contact, including an alternate contact for reports involving
the primary maintainer. That public reporting channel is not yet designated.

## Handling reports and enforcement

Maintainers will review reports in context and seek relevant information before
deciding on action. A person directly involved in a report should not decide its
outcome; an uninvolved Boltzmann Labs maintainer should handle it.

Responses may include clarification, a private warning, removal of harmful
content, temporary participation restrictions, or removal from project spaces.
The response should reflect the seriousness, repetition, and impact of the conduct.

Report details should be shared only with people needed to assess and address the
concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, particularly when action
is necessary to protect someone or disclosure is required by law.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through
the same private channel and provide additional context. Where possible, an
uninvolved maintainer should review that request.
